About Us
Ethical, Precise, and Patient-Centric Surgical Oncology Care

Dr. Varadharajan’s vision is to elevate surgical oncology through ethical, precise, and deeply compassionate care. He believes cancer treatment must go far beyond tumour removal—it should support the patient’s physical comfort, emotional strength, and long-term wellbeing. His approach blends scientific rigor with humanity, ensuring that every individual receives a personalised, evidence-based treatment plan aligned with their diagnosis, stage, lifestyle, and future goals.

His mission is to deliver safe, advanced oncologic surgeries led by early detection, timely decision-making, and coordinated multidisciplinary expertise. He places strong emphasis on survivorship, guiding patients through recovery, recurrence prevention, rehabilitation, and long-term quality-of-life improvement.

He remains academically active, contributing to research, publications, and international oncology conferences. With integrity, empathy, and professionalism at the heart of his practice, Dr. Varadharajan empowers patients and families with clear guidance, honest communication, and the confidence of receiving the highest standards of ethical cancer care

What makes minimally invasive cancer surgery beneficial?

Minimally invasive cancer surgery reduces pain, shortens hospital stay, minimises scarring, and speeds up overall recovery. Dr. Varadharajan V in Chennai uses advanced laparoscopic and robotic systems to enhance surgical precision and limit tissue damage. This approach benefits eligible patients by improving post-operative comfort and reducing complications, without compromising oncologic safety or cure rates. These techniques are especially valuable for gastrointestinal, gynecologic, thoracic, and selected breast cancer surgeries.

What is HIPEC, and who may benefit from it?

HIPEC (Heated Intraperitoneal Chemotherapy) is a specialised treatment used after cytoreductive surgery to target microscopic cancer cells in the abdominal cavity. Dr. Varadharajan V in Chennai performs HIPEC for appropriately selected cases of ovarian, colorectal, gastric, and peritoneal surface cancers. The procedure helps improve survival outcomes in select patients by delivering high-concentration chemotherapy directly where it is needed most, reducing systemic toxicity while enhancing long-term disease control.

Does Dr. Varadharajan perform robotic cancer surgery?

Yes. Dr. Varadharajan V in Chennai is trained in advanced robotic oncosurgery across breast, gastrointestinal, gynecologic, thoracic, and peritoneal cancers. Robotic systems offer magnified 3D vision, greater precision, and minimal tissue trauma. This technology helps improve surgical safety, reduce blood loss, and enable quicker recovery. Patients benefit from smaller incisions, less postoperative discomfort, and faster return to daily activities while maintaining excellent oncologic outcomes.
